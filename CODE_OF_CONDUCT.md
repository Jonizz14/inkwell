# Code of Conduct

Inkwell is a small project made for people who are learning to read in English. Please keep it a
place where anyone, at any level, feels welcome to ask, report and contribute.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good:
- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners and people writing in their second language
- Respecting different opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the app

Not acceptable:
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, releases) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@Jonizz14](https://github.com/Jonizz14), by a
private message on Telegram: [@jonizz_devvvv](https://t.me/jonizz_devvvv). Reports are read and
handled privately. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may ban anyone who keeps doing so.
